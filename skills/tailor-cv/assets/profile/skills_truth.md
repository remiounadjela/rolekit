# Claim guardrails

This file belongs to this candidate. Replace the prompts with confirmed facts.
A job description and an older tailored CV cannot establish a new skill or accomplishment.

## Confirmed skills

List skills you can discuss and demonstrate, with evidence references where available.

## Gaps and claims to avoid

Record missing skills, constraints, and common overstatements to prevent.

## Titles, dates, and scope

Use official titles and exact dates where known. A functional descriptor must accurately describe
work performed; show it as a descriptor rather than a promotion or new official title.
Distinguish direct reports, functional leadership, collaborators, and training reach.
Do not add these counts together or change team size to match job seniority.

## Confirmed accomplishments

Record outcomes, units, time windows, individual contribution, and evidence paths.
Use estimates only with their original qualifier. Unverified results remain unverified.

## Open questions

Resolve conflicting dates, titles, or metrics before using the affected claim in a final CV.
